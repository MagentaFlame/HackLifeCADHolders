# HackLifeCADHolders
This is a keyboard fidget toy! I've always wanted to play with one. 
I initially wanted to make extra charms and make the main toy a "holder" in the back for them, but time constraints didn't allow me lol.
The main board and holder space has geometric surfaces on the top, trees, and a shape resembling "summoner's rift". The sides have HLE's slogan "Beyond the Challenge" and their symbol engraved/embossed.
I added special keycaps for the 5 members of HLE, HLE's symbol, their mascot, a crown representing their win at MSI, a nexus, and their signature orange.
The middle long keyboard shows who I'm rooting for for the LOL World Championships.
